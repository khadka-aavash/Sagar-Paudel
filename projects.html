Research project

Socio-economic barriers to NbS (NL2120)

Identifying the socio-economic and institutional barriers to implementing nature-based solutions in the Netherlands and beyond, with Wetlands International.

  • Wetlands International
  • NL2120

Applied research with Wetlands International on why nature-based solutions that work ecologically still struggle to scale.

NL2120 asks what the Dutch landscape could look like if nature did more of the engineering — wetter peat, wider rivers, hedgerows doing the work of infrastructure. The ecological case is largely settled. This research looks at what is not.

What the work looks at

  • Land tenure, and who holds the right to change how land is used
  • Financing and insurance that cover the transition years, not only the outcome
  • Succession planning, and the gap between who carries the risk and who collects the benefit
  • Institutional and policy pathways that help or block landscape-scale rollout

Policy & advocacy

Global youth advocacy in climate & agriculture

Representing youth perspectives at UN climate platforms (COP27, COP28, SB62, World Food Forum) through IAAS, focusing on multi-stakeholder collaboration and policy advocacy.

  • IAAS
  • World Food Forum

Representing youth perspectives at UN climate platforms through the International Association of Students in Agriculture and Related Sciences (IAAS).

The work centres on multi-stakeholder collaboration: bringing young people, farmers, scientists and negotiators into the same conversation, and making sure that what is said in the room is written down afterwards.

Where it has happened

  • COP27 — Sharm El-Sheikh, Egypt
  • COP28 — Food Systems Pavilion, Dubai
  • UNFCCC SB62 — Bonn, Germany
  • World Food Forum, Youth Assembly — Rome, Italy

The remarks from several of these platforms are on the Speaking & Blog page.

Research project

ML-assisted wetland & land-use mapping

Combining remote sensing, GIS and machine-learning classification to map peatlands and track restoration outcomes at landscape scale.

  • Remote sensing
  • GIS
  • Python

Turning satellite pixels into restoration evidence.

Remote sensing, GIS and machine-learning classification are combined to map peatlands and land use, and to track whether restoration is changing the landscape at scale.

Toolkit

  • Satellite remote sensing
  • GIS analysis
  • Machine-learning classification in Python

For a non-technical walkthrough, read Mapping wetlands with machine learning, plainly on the blog.
